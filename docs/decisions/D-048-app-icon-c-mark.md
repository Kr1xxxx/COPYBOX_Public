### D-048 The app icon is the owner's C mark (2026-10-14)
- Context: D-046 (2026-10-13) shipped a new icon drawn by an agent — a box holding two overlapping cards. The same
  day the owner sent his own 1024 px icon in the logo thread (「这是图标，你优化一下」): a dark gradient squircle, a
  white sideways U (the C of Copyboxx / an open box) with a tilted purple card in its mouth, and approved the vector
  version (「看着不错」). Two icons cannot both be the app icon.
- Decision (owner's picture, 2026-10-14): the **C mark is the app icon** everywhere D-046 lists: `src-tauri/icons/`
  (icon.svg, pixel-fitted icon-16.svg / icon-24.svg, icon.png / .ico / .icns, the installer picture), the website
  (favicon.svg drawn on the 16 px grid, favicon.ico, touch and manifest icons, share card) and the brand files in
  `docs/brand/` (one-colour symbol and icon, wordmark lockups). The design master is `docs/brand/icon.svg`
  (generated by `tools/brand/build-logo.py`); `src-tauri/icons/icon.svg` is a copy in the layout
  `scripts/render-icons.mjs` expects (tile at 100..924 of 1024), and `node scripts/render-icons.mjs` plus
  `node tools/website/make-icons.mjs` regenerate the rest. Palette: tile `#2d2b33`→`#16151b`, C `#ffffff`, card
  `#c4b9ff`→`#8f7ff6`; flat `#1D1C22` / `#A99CFF` in the pixel-fitted sizes.
- Everything else in D-046 (name, installer language, brand sidebar, launch-at-login move) stands; only its icon
  paragraph is superseded.
- Status: Accepted (owner's icon 2026-10-13, approved 2026-10-14). Supersedes the icon in D-046.
