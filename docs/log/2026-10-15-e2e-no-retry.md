# E2E: no CI retry; tests wait for a card to finish opening (audit F)

- Date: 2026-10-15 · Thread/agent: 測試 · 修不穩的折疊測試（審計 F）
- What changed: In the last 60 CI runs, 18 had a test that failed once and passed on retry, spread over about 15 tests
  (save-toast, images, usage-ranking, lazy-save, save-path, shared-library, main-flows), not one collapse test. A trace
  caught on a GitHub runner showed the cause: the test clicked 复制/编辑/删除 inside a card about 60 ms after opening it,
  while the card was still growing (260 ms) and its buttons were still moving down. On a busy runner Playwright saw two
  equal frames, called the button stable, and clicked where it had been, so the click hit the card and did nothing. New
  `openRow(page, rowMain)` in `tests/e2e/helpers.mjs` clicks the title and waits with `settle` until nothing moves; every
  `.row-main` click before acting in a card uses it (the closing click in the mini test and the collapse/layout tests
  that already settle are unchanged). The remembered-value drag test now settles before measuring the grip. With the
  cause fixed, `playwright.config.mjs` has `retries: 0` on CI too. The app itself is unchanged.
- User-visible change (plain Chinese, for the preview changelog): 无
- Not done / risks: The HANDOFF line "main-flows collapse e2e test is flaky (fallback timer)" was wrong about the cause
  and can go when the coordinator next updates HANDOFF. Any new flake now fails CI instead of hiding behind a retry:
  look at the uploaded trace (playwright-report artifact) and fix the cause.
